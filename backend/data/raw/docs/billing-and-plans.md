## Upgrading or downgrading

Plan changes take effect immediately. Upgrades are prorated for the current billing period; downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period, not immediately — you keep access to your current plan's limits until then.

## What happens if I exceed my monthly task limit

Workflows are **not** paused or blocked when you exceed your monthly limit. You'll see a usage warning banner in the dashboard and receive an email at 80% and 100% of your limit. Continued use beyond 100% may result in a prompted upgrade at renewal; Enterprise accounts with overage billing configured are billed per the terms in their contract.

## Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling downgrades your account to the Free plan at the end of the current billing period. If you're on the Business or Enterprise plan and have more than 5 active workflows or more than 1 team member at cancellation time, those workflows are automatically paused (not deleted) until you either upgrade again or manually reduce them to Free-plan limits.

## Invoices and payment methods

Manage billing details, download past invoices, and update your payment method under **Settings → Billing**. Invoices are issued on the 1st of each billing month and are available as PDF downloads for up to 7 years.
